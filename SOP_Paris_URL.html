🔥 Fire / Smoke

  • 1
    Activate the nearest fire alarm. Do not fight the fire unless trained and it is small and safe to do so.
  • 2
    Call Fire Brigade: 18 (or 112)
  • 3
    Notify RSOC: +972 74-723-6000 | Emily Garfen: +33 6 86 31 78 71 | Serge Palaric: +33 6 73 28 39 52
  • 4
    Evacuate via the nearest safe exit – no elevator | Emily Garfen sweeps the floor, last to leave
  • 5
    Assemble in front of the Engie tower | Headcount | Report missing persons
  • 6
    Do not re-enter until explicit clearance from the Fire Brigade

🔫 Security Incident / Workplace Violence – Run · Hide · Fight

📞 Office has no landline — all internal calls route via the Office Manager's mobile: +33 6 86 31 78 71
  • 1
    RUN – Evacuate the area immediately, leave belongings behind
  • 2
    HIDE – If you cannot run: lock/barricade the door, turn off lights, stay low and silent
  • 3
    FIGHT – Only as a last resort, use any available means
  • 4
    Call Police: 17 (or 112) | Notify RSOC: +972 74-723-6000 | Contact ISIS Sécurité (24/7): +33 1 43 33 67 55
  • 5
    Do not attempt solo rescue – wait for security forces, do not open doors until Police confirm the building is clear
⚠️ No panic button confirmed on site — please confirm whether one exists or should be installed.

🎒 Bomb Threat / Suspicious Package

Flagged as a relevant local risk for this site, per the site risk assessment.
  • 1
    Suspicious package: indicators include protruding wires, grease stains, or rigid/bulky envelopes. Do NOT touch, move, or open it.
  • 2
    Phone threat: do not hang up. Note the exact wording and any background noise if it is safe to do so.
  • 3
    Isolate the area and calmly evacuate via the nearest exit — do not use mobile phones or radios near a suspicious item
  • 4
    Assemble in front of the Engie tower
  • 5
    Call Police: 17 (or 112) | Notify Emily Garfen: +33 6 86 31 78 71 | RSOC: +972 74-723-6000
  • 6
    Do not re-enter until Police confirm the building is clear

🛗 Elevator Emergency / Entrapment

  • 1
    Press the alarm button inside the elevator — this alerts building management directly
  • 2
    Call DALKIA (24/7): +33 6 22 17 16 91 or +33 810 804 805
  • 3
    Notify Emily Garfen: +33 6 86 31 78 71 or Serge Palaric: +33 6 73 28 39 52
  • 4
    Stay calm — do NOT attempt to force open the doors. Remain in the elevator until rescue is confirmed.
  • 5
    If medical assistance is needed after rescue, call SAMU: 15
⚠️ During a fire alarm: do NOT use the elevator — use the stairs only.
