🔥 Fire / Smoke

⚠️ This procedure applies to fire/smoke originating within NVIDIA offices – not general building fire alarms
  • 1
    Discover fire/smoke – attempt to extinguish with nearest fire extinguisher only if small and your escape route is clear.
  • 2
    Activate the nearest Fire Alarm pull station. Call Fire Brigade: 101
  • 3
    Notify RSOC: +972 74-723-6000 | Site Leader Dmitry Duka assumes Incident Command | SEC Marina Badalyan: +374 44 417 801 | Office Manager Rita Khachikyan: +374 91 709 970
  • 4
    Evacuate employees via stairs – do NOT use the elevator | Fire Marshals: last to leave, check every room
  • 5
    Assemble at Ani Plaza Hotel front | Conduct headcount | Report missing persons
  • 6
    Prevent re-entry – until official All Clear from emergency services
Fire Extinguisher types on site: Powder – office lobby, restroom zone (all fires). CO2 – NER room (electrical fires only). Never pour water on electrical or flammable liquid fires.

🌍 Earthquake

⚠️ Armenia is in an active seismic zone. Know your safe spots in advance – away from windows and heavy furniture!
  • 1
    During shaking: DROP to the floor, take COVER under a sturdy desk or against an interior wall, HOLD ON
  • 2
    Stay away from windows, exterior walls, shelves, and heavy objects that could fall
  • 3
    Do NOT run outside during shaking – most injuries occur from falling debris near exits
  • 4
    After shaking stops: check for injuries. Provide First Aid if trained. Evacuate via stairs if building may be structurally compromised
  • 5
    Assemble at Ani Plaza Hotel front | Remain outside until building is declared safe
  • 6
    IC reports status to RSOC: +972 74-723-6000 | Report any injuries or structural damage

🔒 Security Threat / Hostile Intruder

📹 Security cameras operate 24/7.  |  👮 A security guard is stationed in the residential area.
  • 1
    Call Police immediately: 102 – provide location and description of threat
  • 2
    RUN – if a safe escape route exists, evacuate immediately. Leave all belongings.
  • 3
    HIDE – lock/barricade doors, silence phones, turn off lights, stay low and out of sight
  • 4
    FIGHT – as an absolute last resort only, use any available object to defend yourself
  • 5
    SEC reports to RSOC: +972 74-723-6000 | Initiate building lockdown via property management
  • 6
    Do NOT open doors until Police confirms the building is clear
