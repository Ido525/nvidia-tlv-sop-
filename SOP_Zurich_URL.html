🔥 Fire / Fire Alarm

  • 1
    On discovering fire: activate nearest pull station immediately — do NOT fight the fire unless trained and safe to do so
  • 2
    Notify Site Leader Adam Moravanszky: +41 79 843 14 35 | Catherine Luke: +41 79 810 81 32 | Elsje Du Preez: +41 79 377 06 80
  • 3
    Evacuate immediately via nearest emergency staircase — do NOT use lifts. Close doors behind you.
  • 4
    Building sweep team performs floor sweep — last to leave. Confirm all rooms clear.
  • 5
    Proceed to assembly area: Kasernenareal (behind building, across 2 roads). Stay together for headcount.
  • 6
    IC reports status to RSOC: +972 74-723-6000. Do NOT re-enter until Fire Brigade declares All Clear.
🧯 CO2 fire extinguishers are located throughout the floor. AED is on the wall near the ERT corner (3rd floor) and in the 4th floor nursing room.

🚑 Medical Emergency

  • 1
    Call ambulance: 144 immediately. Provide: location (Europaallee 39, 3rd/4th floor), nature of emergency, your name.
  • 2
    Notify Site Leader Adam Moravanszky: +41 79 843 14 35 | Catherine Luke: +41 79 810 81 32 | Elsje Du Preez: +41 79 377 06 80
  • 3
    First Aiders: retrieve First Aid Kit + AED from ERT corner (3rd floor, behind OM desk) or 4th floor nursing room
  • 4
    Send someone to building entrance to guide paramedics directly to the person
  • 5
    Clear area of non-essential people. Maintain privacy and dignity of affected person.
  • 6
    IC reports to RSOC: +972 74-723-6000. Complete incident report within 24 hours.
Nearest hospital: Unispital Zürich — Rämistrasse 100, 8091 Zurich — 044 255 11 11
Walk-in clinic: Permanence am Bahnhof — 044 215 44 44

🔒 Security Threat / Hostile Intruder

👮 Securitas security is stationed next door at Europaallee 41 — available 24/7.
  • 1
    Call Police immediately: 117 — provide location and description of threat
  • 2
    RUN — if a safe escape route exists, evacuate immediately. Leave all belongings.
  • 3
    HIDE — lock/barricade doors, silence phones, turn off lights, stay low and out of sight
  • 4
    FIGHT — as an absolute last resort only
  • 5
    SEC reports to RSOC: +972 74-723-6000 | SIOC: +1 408 486 4000 | Contact Securitas: +41 79 544 78 74
  • 6
    Do NOT open doors until Police confirms the building is clear

🛗 Elevator Emergency / Entrapment

  • 1
    Press the alarm button inside the elevator — this alerts building management directly
  • 2
    Call Leonteq Facility Management: Fabian Muff +41 79 544 78 74 | Franco Scandella +41 79 962 44 98
  • 3
    Notify Site Leader Adam Moravanszky: +41 79 843 14 35 or Catherine / Elsje
  • 4
    Stay calm — do NOT attempt to force open doors. Remain in elevator until rescue is confirmed.
  • 5
    If medical assistance needed after rescue, call Ambulance: 144
⚠️ During fire alarm: do NOT use elevators — use emergency staircases only.

📍 Assembly Point

  • 1
    Exit via nearest emergency staircase. Do NOT use lifts.
  • 2
    Proceed to: Kasernenareal — behind the building, across 2 roads
  • 3
    IC / Safety Officers conduct headcount. Report any missing persons immediately.
  • 4
    Remain at assembly point until IC declares All Clear. Do NOT re-enter the building without authorization.
📍 Kasernenareal — Go behind the building and cross 2 roads. Follow evacuation maps posted on all floors.
